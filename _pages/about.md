I received my Ph.D from the Department of Linguistics at McGill University, under the supervision of Michael Wagner and Bernhard Schwarz. My research focuses mainly on formal semantics, pragmatics and syntax. In general though, I am interested in using formal methods in order to analyze the logical and mathematical aspects of natural language. I am also interested in applying computational methods to the study of semantics and, more generally, human cognition as it pertains to language.
